Privacy Policy

Harvey Execution Bridge is a private automation service operated for a single owner's use.

Google user data

The service accesses Google Drive data only to carry out explicit owner-requested operations, such as reading files, verifying file state, creating narrowly scoped records, and producing audit or recovery receipts.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, transferred to unrelated third parties, or used to train generalized machine-learning or AI models.

Operational records may be retained only when needed for security, auditability, recovery, or verification of requested actions. Access credentials are intended to be stored as protected service secrets and are not published in this website or public source repository.

The use of information received from Google Workspace APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Other connected services

Data from other connected services, if any, is processed only within the permissions granted by the owner and only to perform explicitly requested operations.

Questions about this service may be directed to the developer contact address shown in the associated Google OAuth application.
